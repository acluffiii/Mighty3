# Sunovabit: Business Plan

*Southern-built software with grit.*
Draft 1, October 2026 · Texas, USA

---

## 1. Executive summary

Sunovabit is a one-founder Texas technology company that builds iPhone apps and custom software. It launches with two products:

- **DentDOG PDR**: a professional estimating tool for paintless dent repair (PDR) and hail-damage technicians. It's a business-to-business (B2B) product sold as a subscription.
- **Mow Money**: a relaxing, ASMR-style lawn-mowing game. It's a consumer (B2C) product that's free to download, with optional in-app purchases.

Next comes **custom solutions**: apps, websites, and automation for small businesses that still run on paper and spreadsheets.

Startup costs are deliberately tiny (see §9). The founder writes the code, hosting is free, and the main recurring costs are Apple's $99 a year developer fee and a domain name. The goal for the first 12 months is to ship both apps, reach paying DentDOG users, and turn the company story and site into steady custom-project leads.

## 2. Company story, mission and values

**The name.** "Sunovabit" plays on a Southern exclamation. It's spelled so it reads innocently and nobody gets in trouble for saying it, and it ends in *bit*, the smallest unit of computer data. Inside it is *nova*, the exploding star that also appears on the Juneteenth flag.

**Mission.** Build honest, useful software that gives hard-working people their time back.

**Values**, the work ethic our parents taught us:
1. **Do it right.** Finish what you start and sweat the details.
2. **Be straight with people.** Plain-language pricing and privacy policies, no dark patterns.
3. **Open doors.** We honor the civil rights movement's legacy by building tools that make opportunity easier to reach, and by mentoring and hiring locally as we grow.
4. **Stay curious.** We're still the kid taking the computer apart to see how it works.

**Brand.** The Lone Star (Texas), the Juneteenth nova (Galveston, 1865), and circuit traces (technology). The palette is navy, Texas sunset orange, Juneteenth red, and gold. Tagline: *Built with Grit. Coded with Purpose.* **Official mark: Concept A, "Juneteenth Nova".** Logos are in `Brand-Assets/sunovabit/logo/`, the App Store icon is in `Brand-Assets/sunovabit/app-icon/`, and the original concepts are in `Brand-Assets/sunovabit/concepts.html`.

## 3. Products

### 3.1 DentDOG PDR (B2B, iPhone)
**Who it's for:** PDR technicians, hail chasers, and small dent shops who write estimates on hail lots, at dealerships, and in driveways.
**Problem:** Estimates get written on paper hail matrices or clunky desktop tools, then re-typed. That's slow, error-prone, and looks unprofessional.
**Solution:** A panel-by-panel "card deck" estimator that:
- detects dents in photos using Difference-of-Gaussians (DOG) image analysis
- measures dents with photo or dual-camera measuring
- fills in coin-size brackets and pricing automatically
- handles two vehicles at once
- exports a clean PDF on the spot
- includes a Home Screen widget

**Roadmap:** LiDAR dent measuring on Pro iPhones, insurer platform connections (Mitchell, then CCC), team accounts, a shared price matrix, and cloud backup.
**Edge:** Built by someone who knows the trade, phone-first, and fast in the field.

### 3.2 Mow Money (B2C, iPhone game)
**Who it's for:** casual and "cozy" gamers, ASMR fans, and perfectionists who find satisfying, repetitive tasks calming.
**Gameplay:**
- Mow perfect stripes before the timer runs out.
- Upgrade your mower and use power-ups.
- Three level types: residential yards, **world landmarks**, and **highway roadsides** where you dodge animals and cars.

**Why now:** Satisfying, sim-style "job" games and ASMR videos already draw big audiences on TikTok and YouTube. Short clips of perfect stripes are natural marketing.

### 3.3 Custom solutions (services)
Small-business apps, estimate and invoice generators, booking tools, and simple websites. Every app we ship becomes a portfolio piece that sells this service.

Apple takes **15%** of App Store revenue if you join the App Store Small Business Program (for developers earning under $1M a year). Apply as soon as you're enrolled.

## 7. Operations and legal checklist

- [ ] **Form a Texas LLC** ("Sunovabit LLC") with the Texas Secretary of State on SOSDirect (Form 205, **$300**).
- [ ] **Get an EIN** from the IRS. It's free and done online in minutes.
- [ ] **Open a business bank account.**
- [ ] **Get a D-U-N-S number.** It's free; Apple provides a lookup and request tool.
- [ ] **Enroll in the Apple Developer Program as an *Organization*** ($99 a year) so the App Store shows "Sunovabit LLC" instead of your personal name. If you're already enrolled as an individual, Apple can convert the account.
- [ ] **Join the App Store Small Business Program** (15% commission).
- [ ] **Run a trademark search** on USPTO.gov for "SUNOVABIT", "DentDOG", and "Mow Money". Note: a fintech company called "Sunbit" exists. It's a different industry, but the names look alike, so search carefully. Consider filing a federal trademark later (about $350 per class).
- [ ] **Register the domain:** sunovabit.com (about $10–11 a year at Cloudflare Registrar).
- [ ] **Publish privacy policies** (done in `site/`) and keep them updated as the apps change.
- [ ] **Check Texas sales tax:** Apple collects tax on App Store sales; ask a CPA about custom software services.
- [ ] **Keep business and personal money separate.** Track expenses for tax deductions.

## 8. Technology

- **Apps:** Swift and SwiftUI. DentDOG uses AVFoundation (camera), and on-device image processing; builds run through XcodeGen and Codemagic CI. Mow Money is likely SpriteKit, or Unity/Godot if it needs 3D landmarks.
- **Website:** static HTML and CSS. It runs on **Bluehost** (paid; includes the first-year domain, SSL and support@sunovabit.com email) or **GitHub Pages** (free). Upload instructions for both are in `site/README.md`.
- **Email:** iCloud for now; later, support@sunovabit.com through Bluehost email, or through Cloudflare Email Routing (free) if the site is on GitHub Pages.
- **Privacy by design:** data stays on the device, with no third-party tracking by default.

## 11. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| App Store rejection | Complete privacy and support pages, accurate permission prompts, and test on TestFlight first |
| Hail-season swings in DentDOG revenue | Offer annual plans, sell to dealerships and body shops year-round, and grow custom-services revenue |
| One-person bandwidth | Ship small, automate builds (Codemagic), say no to scope creep, and use freelancers for art and sound |
| Name or trademark conflict | USPTO search now; file a trademark once revenue starts |
| Games are a crowded market | Lean into the distinct ASMR and landmark angle, and keep marketing costs near zero with short-form video |

## 12. The ask, for later

Sunovabit is bootstrapped. If outside funding becomes useful, look at the SBA microloan program, Texas small-business grants, and minority-founder programs such as the Minority Business Development Agency (MBDA) business centers and local Black chambers of commerce.
