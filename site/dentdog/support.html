Need help with DentDOG PDR? Send us a message below or check the answers to common questions.

Ask a question, report a problem, or request help using the form below. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and what happened. We usually reply within one to two business days.

Frequently asked questions

Which iPhones does DentDOG support?

DentDOG runs on iPhones with iOS 16 or later.

Why does DentDOG ask for camera access?

The camera is how you photograph the damage on each panel. Photos are processed on your iPhone. If you said no by accident, go to Settings → DentDOG → Camera.

How do I send an estimate to a customer or adjuster?

Tap the share button on the deck. DentDOG makes a PDF of the current vehicle and opens the iOS share sheet so you can AirDrop it, email it, or save it to Files.

How do I switch between two vehicles?

Tilt the deck by swiping up or down on the front card. While it's tilted, keep swiping sideways to switch to the other vehicle.

Does DentDOG connect to Mitchell or CCC?

Not yet. Insurer connections are planned for a future update.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose DentDOG PDR. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. For a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought a subscription but the features are locked. What do I do?

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it, then use Restore Purchases in the app. If it still doesn't unlock, send us a message using the form above.

Is my data backed up?

Estimates are stored on your iPhone. Export a PDF of anything you need to keep. Deleting the app deletes its data.
