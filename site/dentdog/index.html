DentDOG PDR

Hail and dent estimating built for paintless dent repair techs. Work through the car one panel at a time, snap photos, measure dents with your iPhone camera, and send a professional PDF estimate before you leave the lot.

Coming soon to the App Store Get support
Features

From walk-around to estimate, fast.

🃏Panel-by-panel deck

Every panel of the car is a card. Swipe through them, log dent counts and sizes, and watch the estimate total itself.

🔍DOG dent detection

Image analysis using the Difference-of-Gaussians (DOG) method highlights dents in your photos so you miss fewer of them.

📏LiDAR & photo measuring

Measure dent size with LiDAR on Pro iPhones, or with photo and dual-camera measuring on other models. Sizes snap to the familiar dime, nickel, quarter, and half-dollar buckets.

🚗Two vehicles at once

Keep two cars open and switch between them with a swipe. Made for busy hail lots.

📄PDF estimates

Create a clean, itemized PDF and send it by AirDrop, Mail, Messages, or Files.

🗺️Panel map & widget

Tap a vehicle diagram to jump to any panel, or start a new estimate straight from your Home Screen widget.

Connecting to insurer estimating platforms (such as Mitchell and CCC) is planned for a future update. It isn't available yet.
