Our roots

Why "Sunovabit"?

Say it out loud and you'll grin. That's on purpose. It's a little Southern attitude with a nerdy twist: bit, the smallest piece of computer data. Hidden in the middle is nova, an exploding star. Our emblem is built on those ideas.

  • ★

    Texas, first

    The Lone Star sits at the center of everything we make. We're Southern, we're from Texas, and we build like it.

  • ✺

    Juneteenth's nova

    Freedom news reached Galveston, Texas on June 19, 1865. The starburst on the Juneteenth flag, called a nova, frames our star as a reminder of where we come from.

  • ✊

    The march goes on

    We draw on the courage of the civil rights movement: people who stood up, did the work, and opened doors for those who came after. We try to build things that open doors too.

  • ⚒

    Raised on hard work

    Our parents taught us to finish what you start, be honest, and do the job right even when nobody's watching. That's the standard every app ships with.

  • ⌨

    Always the computer kid

    Taking things apart, figuring out how they work, staying up too late on the family PC. That curiosity became a company.

  • 🤝

    People over everything

    Real support from a real person. Email us and you'll hear back from the person who built the app.
