Preload · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Sunovabit ("we," "us") publishes Preload, an iOS game. This policy explains what the app does, and does not do, with your information.

Data we collect

None. Preload does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The app has no account creation, no network connection, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Preload saves two small pieces of information locally on your device only, using Apple's standard app storage: your best score, and your chosen difficulty level. This information never leaves your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Third parties

Preload does not use any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics services.

Children's privacy

Because Preload collects no personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised effective date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact form. If you use the form, we receive your name, email address, and message and use them only to reply.
