PRELOAD

A fast-paced package-sorting arcade game that puts you on the early-morning preload shift. It's a real blue-collar job turned into a game that sharpens your decision-making and reaction time. Read the serial. Load the right shelf. Leave what's not yours alone.

Coming soon to the App Store Get support
Why Preload

Real work. Real skills. Game speed.

Preload is built on the rhythm of a real hub: boxes don't wait, and every call is yours to make. Each shift trains the same quick, careful thinking that the job demands.

🧠Decision-making

Every box is a choice: which shelf, RERUN or AIR, or hands off. Make the right call under pressure, again and again.

⏱️Reaction time

The belt keeps moving and speeds up as you improve. Read fast, act fast, and stay accurate.

🦺Blue-collar roots

Inspired by the real preload shift: serial ranges, misloads, "do not touch" freight, and the pride of a clean belt.

How it works

Every box has a home. Find it fast.

🔢Sort by serial

Packages roll down the belt labeled 1000s, 2000s, 3000s, or 4000s. Load each onto its matching shelf before it rides off the end.

✈️Special cases

Boxes with no label go to RERUN, and boxes marked with an airplane go to AIR.

🚫Leave it alone

"Do Not Touch" packages earn points for discipline. Don't load them; just let them pass.

🔥Build a combo

Chain correct loads for a 2x, 3x, or 4x score multiplier at 5, 10, and 20 in a row.

⚡Power-ups

Grab Slow, Freeze, or Remove Strike boxes to keep a shift alive.

🎚️Three difficulties

Easy, Normal, and Hard change the belt's pace. Everything else stays the same.

🎓Practice Mode

No strikes and a slower belt. Just read and load.

🏆Climb the ranks

Seasonal Helper → Preloader → Lead Loader → Hub Legend.

🔒No data collected

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your best score stays on your phone.
