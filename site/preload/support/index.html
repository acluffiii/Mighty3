How to play

  • Packages roll down the belt showing a serial number. Load each one onto the matching shelf: 1000s, 2000s, 3000s, or 4000s.
  • A box with no label goes to RERUN. A box with an airplane icon goes to AIR.
  • "Do Not Touch" boxes should be left alone. Don't load them; just let them ride off the belt.
  • A shift ends after 3 strikes (wrong shelf, touching a "Do Not Touch" box, or letting a good box ride off untouched) or after 40 packages.
  • Chain correct loads for combo multipliers, and grab power-up boxes (Slow, Freeze, Remove Strike) to stay alive longer.
  • Try Practice Mode for a slower belt with no strikes while you learn the ropes.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change the difficulty?

Tap the gear icon on the home screen before starting a shift.

How do I reset my best score?

Best score is stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app will reset it.

What's Practice Mode?

A slower, strike-free version of a shift, meant for learning the serial ranges and shelf layout.

I found a bug. What should I include in my report?

Your device model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
