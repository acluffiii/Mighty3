OvaYonder · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Sunovabit ("we") makes OvaYonder. This policy describes version 1. Short version: no account, no ads, and no tracking; what you save stays on your phone.

Location

OvaYonder asks for your location while you use it. Your position is used to center the map, plan a driving route, follow a trip, estimate fuel range from GPS miles, and request weather and alerts. During an active trip, location keeps updating if the phone locks so turn prompts can be spoken; that stops when you tap End. OvaYonder has no account system and keeps no location history on a Sunovabit server.

Your coordinate is sent to:

  • Apple, for Maps search, directions, traffic, Apple Weather (WeatherKit), and searches for gas stations and restaurants along a route
  • The National Weather Service (api.weather.gov), as the point for an active-alerts request

The Harris County Flood Warning System, Houston TranStar, ESPN team schedules, and Ticketmaster's Houston event search do not receive your coordinate. Whether a route overlaps an event is worked out on your phone.

Motion

During a trip, if drive insights are on, the app reads the accelerometer and gyroscope to estimate hard braking, quick starts, and sharp turns, and to tell a mounted phone from one held in the hand. Motion samples and trip summaries stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

Saved places and fuel settings

Home, Work, other favorites, recent destinations, fuel settings, and GPS miles are stored on your phone only.

Contacts

If you open People, the app asks to read your contacts. It uses names, postal addresses, phone numbers, and thumbnails, and only offers people who have a postal address. Looked-up addresses are cached on your phone. Messages are written in the Messages app and are only sent when you tap Send; OvaYonder cannot send a message by itself.

Live ETA

Sharing a live ETA is something you turn on for a single trip. The share is a record in OvaYonder's iCloud (CloudKit) container, identified by a random token, holding the destination name, arrival time, remaining distance and time, an approximate location (rounded to about a kilometer), and the route score. It updates about once a minute and stops when you arrive, tap End or Stop, or after four hours, and the record is then deleted. We keep no cloud history of the trip. Anyone you send the link to can follow it until it stops.

Other data

Road, gauge, and event readings come from public sources. Those requests don't include your name or a device identifier.

Tracking and ads

OvaYonder does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it does not show ads.

Children

OvaYonder is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact form. If you use the form, we receive your name, email address, and message and use them only to reply.
