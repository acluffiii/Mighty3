Frequently asked questions

What do Clear, Caution, and Avoid mean?

They score the route on the map against flood gauges, TranStar closures and high water, and weather alerts. Avoid means a flood-level gauge, high water, a total closure, or a serious warning is on or near the route. Caution means something is rising or partly closed. Clear means none of the rules fired. It does not mean the road is open or safe. Tap "How this score works" on the route card for every cutoff.

Does OvaYonder work outside Houston?

Version 1 is built for Houston and Harris County. The map, search, and directions work anywhere, but flood gauges, TranStar closures, and event traffic only cover the Houston area.

Why does OvaYonder need my location?

To show where you are, plan a route, follow your trip, estimate fuel range, and get weather and alerts for your area. During an active trip it keeps updating if your phone locks so turns can still be spoken. That stops when you tap End.

The weather card shows an error.

Check that you have a data connection, then tap refresh. National Weather Service alerts can still load even when Apple Weather is briefly unavailable.

Why are some gauge pins gray?

Gray (slate) pins are USGS readings with no published flood stage. They are shown for context and don't change the route score.

How does live ETA sharing work?

Start a trip and choose Share live ETA. OvaYonder opens a text with a link; you tap Send. The person you send it to can follow your trip until you arrive, tap End or Stop, or four hours pass. The shared trip is then deleted. Both phones need to be signed in to iCloud.

How accurate is the fuel range?

It's an estimate from GPS miles and the tank and mpg you set, not the car's own gauge. Refuel in the app when you fill up so it can learn your real mpg.

Drive insights didn't record anything.

Insights need a phone that's mounted, not in your hand, and a trip with some normal speeding up and slowing down. Turn them on with the steering-wheel button before you start.
