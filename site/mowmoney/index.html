Mow Money

The most satisfying lawn you'll ever cut. Line up perfect stripes, get every last blade before the clock runs out, and relax to the hum of the mower. Mow Money is easy to pick up and hard to put down. It's made for perfectionists, fidgeters, and anyone who needs to unwind.

Gameplay

Cut it clean. Cut it fast. Cut it everywhere.

🎧ASMR calm

Soft engine hum, fresh-cut grass, and crisp stripes appearing behind you. Made to help you breathe out.

📐Perfect stripes

Score points for straight lines, clean edges, and missing zero blades. Perfectionists, this one's for you.

⏱️Beat the clock

Finish every yard within the time limit, and do it efficiently, for top payouts.

🏡Neighborhood yards

Classic backyards with flower beds, gnomes, sprinklers, and dog toys to steer around.

🗽World landmarks

Mow the lawns at famous landmarks around the world. Bucket list, but make it grass.

🛣️Highway mode

Cut the roadside while dodging deer, armadillos, traffic, and other hazards.

⚡ Upgrades & power-ups

Spend what you earn on wider decks, faster engines, and power-ups that get the job done quicker. Then go back and beat your best time.
