Frequently asked questions

Which devices can play Mow Money?

Mow Money is being built for iPhone. We'll post exact device requirements here at launch.

My progress disappeared. What happened?

Game progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app, or restoring your phone without a backup, can erase it. Send us a message with your device model and we'll help however we can.

I bought an upgrade but don't see it.

Purchases go through Apple. Try the Restore Purchases button in Settings. If it still doesn't show up, send us a message with your order ID from Apple's receipt email (no card numbers, please).

How do I turn the sound or vibration off?

Open the in-game Settings menu. You can adjust music, sound effects, and haptics separately.

I have an idea for a level or landmark!

We'd love to hear it. Send us your idea through the contact form, along with the landmarks you'd most like to mow.
