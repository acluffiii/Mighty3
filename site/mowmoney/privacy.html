Mow Money

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Sunovabit ("we") makes Mow Money ("the game"). We built it to be relaxing, and that includes not worrying about your data.

Information we collect

None. Mow Money does not require an account. It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or photos, and it does not use third-party analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, coins, upgrades, and settings are saved on your device so you can pick up where you left off. If you use Apple's Game Center or iCloud, Apple handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, Apple processes the payment. We never see your payment details.

Contacting support

If you send us a message through our website's contact form, we receive your name, email address, and message and use them only to reply to you.

If a future version adds ads, analytics, or online features, we'll update this policy first and explain what changed.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Mow Money handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Sunovabit, Texas, USA. Reach us through our contact form.
