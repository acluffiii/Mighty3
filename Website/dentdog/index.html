DentDOG by Thumbit

Every panel. Every dent. One thumb.

DentDOG turns a hail-damaged vehicle into a stack of panel cards. Swipe through them, take a photo of each one, size the dents with your camera, and send a PDF estimate straight from the lot.

What it does

Built for hail season

DentDOG replaces the clipboard, the paper rate card, and the camera roll full of unlabeled photos.

01

A card for every panel

13 damage panels and 7 required documentation photos. Each one is a card in a deck you swipe through with your thumb, and you can put them in whatever order you walk the car.

02

Double-tap to shoot

Double-tap a card to open the camera, take the photo, and it's attached to that panel. No sorting photos later.

03

Measure dents with your camera

Put a 1" magnet or a standard card next to the dent for scale, and DentDOG measures the dent with your camera. Sizes are matched to dime, nickel, quarter, and half-dollar.

04

Pricing as you go

Choose the dent count bracket, size, and markups like aluminum or glue pull. The price for each panel updates right away, and roof panels are priced separately.

05

PDF estimates

Create a clean, itemized PDF and send it with AirDrop, Mail, Messages, or Files using the normal iOS share sheet.

06

Two vehicles at once

Keep two estimates open and switch between them with a tilt and a swipe. There's also a panel map for jumping to any panel, and a Home Screen widget that starts a new estimate.
