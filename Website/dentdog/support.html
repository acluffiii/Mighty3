Support

We're here to help.

Contact us: email support@thumbit.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. We usually reply within two business days.

Requirements

  • iPhone running iOS 16 or later.
  • LiDAR measurement needs an iPhone with a LiDAR Scanner (iPhone 12 Pro and later Pro models). Every other iPhone can measure dents using a 1" magnet or a standard ID-sized card for scale.
  • The Home Screen widget needs iOS 17 or later.

Common questions

How do I attach a photo to a panel?

Double-tap the panel's card to open the camera and take the photo. To use a photo you already took, tap Import Photo at the top of the screen. It's added to whichever panel is on top of the deck.

How do I measure a dent?

On a panel card, tap Measure dent. On LiDAR iPhones, hold the phone steady and aim at the dent. On other iPhones, put a 1" round magnet or a standard card (like a credit card) flat on the panel next to the dent. DentDOG uses it for scale and matches the dent to dime, nickel, quarter, or half-dollar size.

How do I send an estimate?

Tap the share button at the top right of the deck. DentDOG creates a PDF and opens the iOS share sheet so you can send it with Mail, Messages, or AirDrop, or save it to Files.

How do I work on two vehicles at once?

Swipe up or down on the front card to tilt the deck. While it's tilted, keep swiping sideways to switch vehicles.

DentDOG says it can't use the camera.

Go to Settings › DentDOG and turn on Camera. Then close DentDOG completely and open it again.

Where is my data stored? Can you recover a lost estimate?

Everything stays on your iPhone. Thumbit has no copy, so we can't recover estimates. Export a PDF of any estimate you need to keep. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

You don't have an account with us, so there's nothing to close. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.
