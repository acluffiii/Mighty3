Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026 · Last updated September 24, 2026

Short version: DentDOG keeps your estimates and photos on your iPhone. Thumbit does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information. The app has no account sign-up, ads, analytics, or tracking.

This policy explains how Thumbit ("Thumbit," "we," "us") handles information in the DentDOG iPhone app (the "App") and on this website. If you have questions, email support@thumbit.com.

1. What we collect

We don't collect personal information through the App. DentDOG doesn't ask you to make an account, and it doesn't send your estimates, photos, vehicle details, measurements, location, or device identifiers to Thumbit servers. We don't run servers for the App.

If you email us for support, we get your email address and whatever you put in the message. We only use it to answer you, and we delete support emails when they're no longer needed.

2. What stays on your device

DentDOG creates and stores the following on your iPhone:

  • Estimate details you enter, such as panel dent counts, dent sizes, markups, and prices.
  • Photos you take or import into an estimate.
  • App preferences, such as the order of your panel cards.
  • Sign-in tokens for estimating platforms you choose to connect (see section 5). These are kept in the iOS Keychain.

Thumbit can't see or access any of this. If you delete the App, its data is deleted from your device. Data inside the App may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, depending on your iOS settings. Apple's privacy policy covers those backups.

3. Camera and photos

  • Camera. DentDOG asks for camera access so you can take photos of vehicle panels and measure dents. Camera frames are processed on your device in real time. They are not recorded or sent anywhere. The only thing saved is a photo you choose to capture.
  • Photo library. When you import a photo, DentDOG uses Apple's system photo picker. The App only gets the one photo you pick and can't see the rest of your library.

You can turn off camera access at any time in Settings › DentDOG. Features that need the camera won't work while it's off.

4. When you share estimates

When you export a PDF estimate, DentDOG opens the standard iOS share sheet. You choose where the file goes, for example Mail, Messages, AirDrop, or Files. Whoever you send it to handles it under their own policies. The PDF only goes where you send it, and Thumbit never gets a copy.

5. Estimating platform connections (optional)

DentDOG may let you connect to third-party estimating platforms such as Mitchell or CCC. This is optional. If you connect, you sign in on the provider's own site through Apple's secure web sign-in. Thumbit never sees your password. The access token the provider issues is stored in your iPhone's Keychain and is used only to talk directly to that provider. Anything exchanged with a provider is covered by that provider's privacy policy. You can disconnect at any time in the App, which deletes the stored token.

6. Ads, analytics, and tracking

DentDOG has no advertising, no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no tracking. We don't use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't sell or share personal information for any purpose.

If you have turned on Share with App Developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us crash reports and usage statistics without your name attached. Apple controls this setting and its privacy policy applies.

7. Children

DentDOG is a professional tool made for adults working in the auto repair and insurance industries. It is not made for children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts, and it doesn't load content from third parties. Like any web server, our hosting provider may keep standard access logs, such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, for a short time for security and reliability. We don't use those logs to identify you.

9. Your choices and rights

Your DentDOG data is on your device and under your control. You can edit or delete it in the App, or delete the App to remove all of it. Depending on where you live (for example California, under the CCPA/CPRA, or the EU, UK, or Switzerland, under the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or get a copy of personal information about you. Because we don't collect personal information through the App, we usually have nothing to provide. If you've emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, write to support@thumbit.com.

10. Changes and contact

If we change how DentDOG handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. If we ever start collecting personal information, we'll update our App Store privacy label and ask for your permission where the law requires it.

Questions or requests: support@thumbit.com
